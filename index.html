02 / ABOUT ME

Turning spatial data into meaningful insights.

  • Spatial Data Engineering
  • Geospatial Analysis
  • Data Visualization

I'm a Spatial Data Engineer & Geospatial Analyst working in the forestry sector, passionate about spatial analysis, geospatial technologies, and transforming complex data into meaningful information.

My background is in Environmental Sciences, followed by a Master's degree in Spatial Engineering at the University of Twente in the Netherlands, where I worked on projects involving spatial analysis, geographic modelling, and geospatial technologies.

Today, I combine spatial data engineering, data analysis, and process automation to integrate different sources of information, build efficient data workflows, and develop tools that support better decision-making in forestry management.

Beyond my professional work, I enjoy experimenting with new datasets, exploring spatial patterns, and creating maps that tell stories. This portfolio is a collection of those explorations.
