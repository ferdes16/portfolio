#30DAYMAPCHALLENGE / 2026 / DAY 06 / VINTAGE

Almería in pastel red

A vintage-inspired city portrait.

Streets, buildings and the geometry of the harbour trace Almería beside the Mediterranean. Pastel red, warm cream and a geometric Futura title give contemporary map data the feel of an urban print.

Location
Almería, Spain
Challenge
Day 06 · Vintage
Tools
R · sf · ggplot2 · ragg
Data
OpenStreetMap · October 2026
VIEW FULL IMAGE ↗ DOWNLOAD PDF ↓

The idea

Let the urban fabric carry the composition: the irregular streets of the historic centre, the surrounding street grid and the strong outline of the port. The map explores the visual identity of the city through a restrained print-inspired design.

How it was made

OpenStreetMap extracts were deduplicated, projected to ETRS89 / UTM zone 30N and clipped in R. Road hierarchy controls line width, buildings add subtle texture, and the coastline separates land from sea. Small display-only curve adjustments soften angular roads and roundabouts while retaining connection nodes.

Sources & limitations

Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, downloaded 30 October 2026. This is contemporary mapping with vintage styling, not a historical reconstruction. Coverage varies, the crop shows part of the city, and relation-only buildings may be omitted. Smoothed lines are for display, not routing or surveying.
