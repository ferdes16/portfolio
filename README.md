## About

I'm a Spatial Data Engineer & Geospatial Analyst working in the forestry sector. My background combines Environmental Sciences with a Master's degree in Spatial Engineering from the University of Twente.

I work with spatial data integration, analysis and process automation, and enjoy turning complex geographic information into clear, thoughtful visualizations.

## Projects

### #30DayMapChallenge

A growing collection of maps exploring open data, geographic patterns and different approaches to cartographic design. Each map has a short project page with its idea, methods, tools, sources and limitations.

**[Browse the map gallery →](https://ferdes16.github.io/challenge/)**

- **[Day 06 · Vintage — Almería in pastel red](https://ferdes16.github.io/maps/2026/day-06-vintage/)**: a contemporary city portrait inspired by vintage prints, combining streets, building footprints and the harbour in a restrained pastel palette. Created in R using OpenStreetMap data.
